# slot. for KONKR Pocket Advance — v1.1.0

Based on the original **[slot. v1.7.0](https://slot-cfw.fyi/)** by Brandon Kowalski, this unofficial Android port brings the authentic slot. experience to the KONKR Pocket Advance, with additional features:

- **Native Android support**, optimized for the KONKR's display and physical controls.
- **RetroAchievements**, with automatic background caching and offline unlocks.
- **Wi-Fi multiplayer**, supporting Link Cable and Wireless RFU on compatible games.
- **RetroArch & Syncthing compatibility** for saves and save states.
- **Expanded customization**, including additional shaders and per-system screen settings.
- **Integrated Cart Studio** and Android app launcher.
- **Performance and battery optimizations**, preserving smooth cartridge browsing.

## Installation & Documentation

Download the ARM64 APK below.

For installation instructions, hotkeys, RetroAchievements and multiplayer setup, please refer to the **[README](https://github.com/Baggio94/slot-for-konkr#readme)**.
